Amina’s Story: A Future in the Balance

Amina has always been an exceptional student, ranking among the top in her class. She dreams of one day wearing a doctor’s coat and saving lives. But poverty stands in her way.

Coming from a low-income family, paying school fees, buying textbooks, and affording a uniform has become an overwhelming burden. Despite her efforts, she has already missed several classes due to financial struggles. If she doesn’t get help soon, she may have to drop out permanently.

Education is Amina’s only chance to break the cycle of poverty and change her future.

With your support, she can stay in school, excel in her studies, and work toward becoming the doctor she dreams of becoming.
