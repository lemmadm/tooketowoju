Welcome to TookeOlutowo Foundation (T.O.F.)

At TookeOlutowo Foundation (T.O.F.), we are on a mission to uplift and empower individuals who need it most—people living with sickle cell disease, individuals battling drug addiction, and the girl child fighting for her place in society.

We don’t just offer support. We create opportunities, restore hope, and rewrite stories.

Join us. Be the change.

Who We Are

 “For me, this is not about ‘giving back’—it’s about giving what was never given to me. Through T.O.F., I want to serve, support, and uplift people, striving for the betterment of humanity as a whole.”

TookeOlutowo Foundation was founded by Faisha Oluwatowoju Adeniji, a woman who has walked the very path she now seeks to transform. Living with sickle cell disease, she understands the pain, stigma, and challenges faced by warriors like herself.

She also recognizes the struggles of young girls fighting for recognition and the silent battles of those overcoming addiction.

What We Do
Sickle Cell Awareness & Support

Providing counseling, medical aid, and educational resources for individuals with sickle cell disease.

Drug Rehabilitation & Recovery

Providing pathways for addicts to heal, reintegrate, and rebuild their futures.

Girl-Child Empowerment

Mentorship programs, scholarships, and skills training to build strong, confident young women.

Community Outreach & Advocacy

Hosting workshops, awareness campaigns, and partnering with organizations to extend our impact.

Impact Timeline

We believe in consistent, purpose-driven action. Here’s how TookeOlutowo Foundation shows up all year long to create awareness, support communities, and inspire change:

September – Sickle Cell Awareness Month

Throughout September, we dedicate each day to highlighting the challenges, triumphs, and stories of individuals living with sickle cell disease.

October 11 – International Day of the Girl Child

On this global day, we raise our voices for the girl child—challenging the systems that silence her and amplifying the need for education, safety, and dignity.

December – Full Plate Initiative

As the year closes, we launch our annual Full Plate Initiative—a campaign focused on feeding families, restoring dignity, and spreading love to the underserved.
