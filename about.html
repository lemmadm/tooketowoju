About TookeOlutowo Foundation (T.O.F.)

At TookeOlutowo Foundation (T.O.F.), we believe in empowering the vulnerable, restoring hope, and transforming lives.

Our Story

T.O.F. was founded by Faisha Oluwatowoju Adeniji, a woman who has lived through the struggles she now seeks to ease. Born with sickle cell disease, she understands the pain, stigma, and isolation that often come with it. She also recognizes the silent battles of individuals struggling with addiction and the unrelenting fight of young girls to claim their rightful place in society.

“Helping people has always been an integral part of who I am. This is not about ‘giving back’ but about giving what was never given to me. Through T.O.F., I want to serve, support, and uplift people, striving for the betterment of humanity as a whole.”

— Faisha Oluwatowoju Adeniji, Founder, T.O.F.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide hope, healing, and empowerment for sickle cell warriors, individuals recovering from addiction, and young girls striving for a better future.

Vision: A world where every individual—regardless of their struggles—has the opportunity to thrive, be heard, and live a life of dignity.

Our Core Values

  • Empathy – We deeply understand and prioritize the needs of our beneficiaries.
  • Inclusivity – Everyone deserves a chance, regardless of their background or struggles.
  • Empowerment – We provide individuals with tools to break free from limitations.
  • Integrity – Transparency, accountability, and honesty guide everything we do.

What We Do

Sickle Cell Awareness & Support

Providing medical aid, counseling, and advocacy. | Educating communities to reduce stigma.

Drug Addiction Recovery & Rehabilitation

Helping individuals recover through mentorship, therapy, and reintegration programs. | Partnering with rehabilitation centers to provide holistic care.

Girl Child Empowerment

Sponsoring education, vocational training, and mentorship. | Fighting for equal rights and protection for young girls.

Community Outreach & Advocacy

Organizing workshops, awareness campaigns, and collaborative programs. | Partnering with local and international organizations to extend our impact.
