Get Involved – Be the Change

At TookeOlutowo Foundation (T.O.F.), we believe that real change begins when people come together. Whether you want to volunteer, donate, partner, or simply lend your voice, there’s a place for you here.

Let’s work together to support people living with sickle cell, empower young girls, and restore lives from addiction.

Join the Movement Donate Online

We rise by lifting others. Your hands and heart can help rewrite someone’s story.

Volunteer – Give Your Time & Skills
Passionate about helping others? Whether you’re a doctor, therapist, teacher, counselor, or simply someone who cares, we need people like you to:
Help Someone
  • Assist in sickle cell awareness campaigns & medical outreach.
  • Help mentor and educate young girls.
  • Support drug rehabilitation programs & reintegration efforts.
  • Organize fundraisers & community events.

Donate – Every Contribution Matters

Your donation helps provide lifesaving medical care, education, and recovery support to those in need. You can:

✅ Make a one-time donation to support our programs.
✅ Become a monthly donor and sustain our long-term efforts.
✅ Sponsor an individual – directly fund the needs of someone in crisis.

Partner With Us – Let’s Make a Bigger Impact

Are you a business, organization, or influencer looking to support a meaningful cause? Partnering with T.O.F. means:
  • Funding education, healthcare, and rehabilitation programs.
  • Co-hosting awareness events and outreach programs.
  • Collaborating on advocacy and policy changes.

Together, we can reach more people and create lasting change.

 Spread the Word – Be an Advocate

Not everyone can donate or volunteer, but everyone can raise awareness. You can:
  • Follow us on social media – Share our mission with your network.
  • Start a fundraiser – Rally your friends and family to support T.O.F.
  • Tell your story – If you or someone you know has benefited from our cause, sharing your journey can inspire others to help.
