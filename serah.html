Sarah’s Journey with Sickle Cell Disease

Sarah was diagnosed with sickle cell disease at birth, and since then, her life has been filled with hospital visits, blood transfusions, and excruciating pain. Her mother struggles to afford the costly treatment, and without consistent medical care, Sarah is at risk of life-threatening complications.

Despite her challenges, Sarah remains hopeful and full of life. She loves school and dreams of becoming a doctor so she can help children like herself one day.

With your help, we can give Sarah a future full of possibilities.
