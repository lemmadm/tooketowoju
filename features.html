Who We Support
Passionate about helping others? Whether you’re a doctor, therapist, teacher, counselor, or simply someone who cares, we need people like you to:
Become a Volunteer
  • People Living with Sickle Cell Disease - Access to medical care, counseling, and financial aid for treatment. Awareness programs to reduce stigma and improve healthcare access.
  • Individuals Battling Drug Addiction - Rehabilitation programs and support for addiction recovery. Counseling, mentorship, and reintegration programs.
  • The Girl Child - Scholarships and educational support for young girls in need. Mentorship and vocational training to ensure a brighter future.
